A smart book price analysis tool that scrapes real-time book data and helps you find affordable reads within your budget.

## ✨ Features

- 📊 **Real-time Scraping** - Fetches live book data from books.toscrape.com
- 💰 **Budget Filtering** - Find books within your price range
- 🎛️ **Interactive Slider** - Adjust your budget easily (£10 - £60)
- 📱 **Clean Interface** - Beautiful and responsive design
- ⚡ **Fast Analysis** - Quick book price comparison
- 📈 **Total Book Count** - See how many books were analyzed
